# antislop audit 005 (27 Nov 2026)

Mode: DURING (session choice). Scope: whole web app (landing, public pages, dashboard).
Status per item: FIXED, HELD (owner decision), OPEN (not verified or not in this round).

Owner request: the blocks that identify the design are gone from the dashboard; some buttons and the header are still round while others are square; make the whole UI 20% smaller. Decisions: balance as three data blocks only (no score blocks, no status squares); every corner 0px on the whole site; scale applies to the whole site; controls shrink with it on desktop; phones stay at 100%; page width stays 1280px.

| No | Priority | Rule | Location | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MEDIUM | Brand consistency | Dashboard | The plain redesign (audit 004) removed every block, so the square motif disappeared. Balance is now three square data blocks (`CreditLine`, Overview `Balance`) sharing 1px hairlines. No accent fill, no score blocks. | FIXED |
| 2 | HIGH | Consistency | Whole site | Mixed corners: buttons `999px`, fields `8px`, toggle round, cards `12px`, menus, dialogs, avatars, status dots, skeletons, token images, `sq-index` `3px`. `--radius` is `0` and every `rounded-*` and `border-radius` was removed. Measured at 1440px on `/check`: 0 elements with a corner radius. | FIXED |

Fixed sizes were converted to `rem` (`sq-index`, button and field heights, text 11/15/17px). Container queries use `px` because `rem` there would follow the smaller root. | FIXED |
